Recover sessions after container restarts

## Parent

Project specification: #1

## What to build

Recover persisted recording intent and footage after graceful shutdown or process/container failure, resuming eligible captures and safely recovering interrupted MP4 finalization.

Implement this as an end-to-end slice, including persistent state, API, interface, and observable tests where applicable. Parent specification #1 governs the confirmed requirements; do not close or edit it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Graceful shutdown preserves intent and recoverable media rather than marking the session operator-stopped.
- [ ] On restart, unfinished manual capture resumes against its original source; timed capture resumes only until its original absolute deadline.
- [ ] If the deadline passed during downtime, finalize captured footage without opening a new capture.
- [ ] Retain original session ID, start-time filename, interruptions, and snapshotted source across recovery.
- [ ] Interrupted finalization can be recovered or retried without exposing partial MP4 downloads or duplicating completed recordings.
- [ ] Operator-stopped sessions never resume; admission stays occupied through recovery and processing, with no duplicate live recorder processes.
- [ ] UI and API display recovery/interruption status and actionable unrecoverable errors while preserving salvageable media.
- [ ] Integration tests restart and forcibly terminate capture and processing, verify manual and timed behavior including expired deadlines, and inspect final playable outputs.
